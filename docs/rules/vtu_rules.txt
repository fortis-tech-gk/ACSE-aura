AMENDMENTS (decided by the Organising Committee, applied to the website):
- 07-11-2026: Reserve rule changed — an athlete may nominate 1 reserve event (free) even when registered for both Event 1 and Event 2. The reserve must be a different event and needs at least one regular event (Rule 36). This replaces "after 2 registered reserved cant be used".
- 07-11-2026: Relay substitutes can be changed by the call room official up to 24 hours before the race start.
- 07-11-2026: Relay points (10-6-2) count only for the Overall College Championship; 4x400m Mixed Relay earns no championship points.
- 17-11-2026: Clarified — the ATHLETE scores 5-3-1 for an individual 1st/2nd/3rd; the COLLEGE team scores 10-6-2 for a 1st/2nd/3rd in any event (individual or relay). Colleges are ranked by total team points. Relay points still count only for the Overall championship; Mixed Relay scores nothing. (migration 0042)
- 25-11-2026: Points changed (replaces 07-11 relay rule and 17-11). College championships (Overall, Best Men's College, Best Women's College): individual events 5-3-1; combined events (Decathlon, Heptathlon) and relays 10-6-2. Overall = every event; Men's / Women's College = that division's events, relays included. Mixed Relay 10-6-2 for the Overall Championship only, not the Men's / Women's College (website rules 7 and 14; replaces 07-11 "Mixed Relay earns no points"). (migration 0048)
- 25-11-2026: Best Male / Female Athlete = World Athletics Scoring Tables (2025) points of the athlete's best single performance in a FINAL (the final counts even if the heat was faster; combined events score their total). Ties: second-best performance. (migration 0048)
- 25-11-2026: Time qualifiers allowed (replaces "top 2 from each heat, no time qualifiers"). First round: first 2 of each heat (Q) + fastest losers (q) fill the lanes of the next round (6 heats → 12 Q + 4 q → 2 semi-finals). Semi-finals → final as World Athletics: 2 semis 3 Q + 2 q, 3 semis 2 Q + 2 q, 4 semis 2 Q. Next round seeded and lanes drawn by WA ranking (winners, then 2nd places, …, then q); best 4 in lanes 3–6.
- 25-11-2026: First round roll call — every entrant of the event reports to the call room first; the call room official prepares the heats from the athletes present and they go straight to the referee. Absent = DNS. (migration 0049)
